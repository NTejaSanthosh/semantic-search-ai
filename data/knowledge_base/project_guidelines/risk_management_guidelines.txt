RISK MANAGEMENT GUIDELINES

Document Type: Project Guideline
Category: Risk Management

Project risks should be identified, reviewed, and monitored throughout the project lifecycle.

Risk management guidelines:

1. Identify risks that can affect project scope, schedule, quality, resources, or delivery.

2. Critical risks should receive priority attention.

3. Risks associated with overdue critical tasks should be reviewed promptly.

4. The Project Manager should identify the likely cause of significant risks.

5. Mitigation actions should be defined for important risks.

6. Risk status should be reviewed after mitigation actions are implemented.

7. Unresolved critical risks should be escalated according to the Project Escalation Policy.

8. Risk analysis should use current project information whenever available.

TEAM WORKLOAD GUIDELINES

Document Type: Team Guideline
Category: Employee Workload

Workload should be monitored to reduce delivery risks and uneven task distribution.

Guidelines:

1. Project Managers should review employee workload when projects contain multiple overdue tasks.

2. Employees with a high number of active tasks should be reviewed for possible workload constraints.

3. Task reassignment may be considered when workload imbalance is affecting delivery.

4. Critical tasks should be assigned to appropriate employees based on skills and availability.

5. Workload information should be considered together with project deadlines and task priorities.

6. Workload alone should not be treated as proof of project failure. It should be considered along with current project and task information.
